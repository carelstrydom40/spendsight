Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Draft: to be checked before paid launch

MoneyScope is built so that your financial information stays with you. This policy explains what that means, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

MoneyScope ("we") is operated by Carel Strydom (sole proprietor), South Africa. The responsible party under POPIA is the operator named here. Contact: moneyscope.help@gmail.com.

Your bank statements

Statements you add are read and analysed inside your own browser, on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. If you choose "Remember my statements on this device", they are kept only in that browser's storage. You can remove them at any time in the app (Upload statements → untick "Remember"), or by clearing your browser data.

Optional online lookups

If you switch on "Online Enhanced Analysis", the app may look up the name of a shop it doesn't recognise (for example "Joe's Bakery") on public map services to suggest a category. Amounts, dates, account numbers and your identity are never sent. This is off unless you turn it on.

Account and payment information

If you subscribe, payments are handled by Paddle.com, our online reseller and merchant of record, which receives your payment details directly. We receive only what we need to manage your subscription: your email address, plan and payment status. We keep this for as long as you have a subscription, and afterwards as required by tax law.

Website visits

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses for security. We do not use advertising trackers.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Email moneyscope.help@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell subscribers by email.

Questions: moneyscope.help@gmail.com
