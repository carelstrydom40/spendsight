Questions

Good to know.

Which banks does it work with?

Almost any bank in the world that lets you download a statement: CSV, Excel, PDF, OFX/QIF, or a clear screenshot. MoneyScope recognises the layout, language and currency itself and, if it's ever unsure, asks you once.

Is my information safe?

Your statements are read inside your own browser and are not uploaded to us. We never ask for a bank login. You can delete anything you've chosen to remember, at any time.

How accurate is it?

Every statement is checked against your bank's own running balance, and you're told if anything doesn't add up. You can correct any category, and MoneyScope remembers it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It works like an app, even offline.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in two clicks, from the link in your receipt email or the "Manage or cancel" link in the app. Cancel during the free trial and you pay nothing. Cancel later and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Which currency am I charged in?

Plans are priced in US dollars: $5.99 a month or $59.90 a year. Your bank converts it to your own currency; some banks add a small foreign-transaction fee. MoneyScope itself reads statements in any currency.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. MoneyScope reads statements from banks around the world, in many languages, date formats and number styles. If a layout is new to it, it asks you once which column is which, and remembers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your home screen if you'd like it to feel like an app.

Can I delete my data?

Your statements live only on your device. Untick "Remember my statements" or clear your browser data and they're gone. We never had a copy.

Is this financial advice?

No. MoneyScope shows facts from your own statements and general guidance about money habits. It is not financial advice. For advice about your own situation, speak to a licensed financial adviser where you live.
