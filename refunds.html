Refund policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Draft: to be checked before paid launch

We want you to be happy with MoneyScope. This policy explains trials, cancellations and refunds.

Free trial

Every new subscription starts with a 10-day free trial. Cancel any time during the trial and you will not be charged.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the link in your receipt email or the "Manage or cancel" link in the app, or by emailing moneyscope.help@gmail.com. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Refunds

If you are not happy, ask for a refund within 14 days of a payment and we will refund that payment in full: monthly or yearly, no questions asked. After 14 days, payments are not refundable, except where the law requires otherwise. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected.

How refunds are paid

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, who is the merchant of record for all our orders and handles refunds. Refunds go back to the card or PayPal account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 working days. Email moneyscope.help@gmail.com to request one.

Questions: moneyscope.help@gmail.com
