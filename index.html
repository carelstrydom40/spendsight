Privacy by design

Your statements never leave your device.

Most money apps ask for your bank login or send your statements to their servers. MoneyScope works the other way around: all the reading and adding up happens inside your own browser.

No bank login

We never ask for your banking password and never connect to your bank.

Processed on your device

Your statements are read in your browser, not uploaded. The app even works with your Wi-Fi off.

You stay in control

Nothing is kept unless you choose to remember it on your device, and you can delete it any time. Optional online lookups send only a shop's name, never amounts or account details.

Questions

Good to know.

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank that lets you download a statement: Capitec, FNB, Standard Bank, Absa, Nedbank, Discovery, TymeBank, African Bank and others. MoneyScope recognises the layout itself and, if it's ever unsure, asks you once.

Is my information safe?

Your statements are read inside your own browser and are not uploaded to us. We never ask for a bank login. You can delete anything you've chosen to remember, at any time.

How accurate is it?

Every statement is checked against your bank's own running balance, and you're told if anything doesn't add up. You can correct any category, and MoneyScope remembers it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It works like an app, even offline.

Is this financial advice?

No. MoneyScope shows facts from your own statements and general guidance about money habits. It is not financial advice as defined in the FAIS Act. Speak to a registered financial adviser for advice about your situation.
