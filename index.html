How do I get a CSV statement from my bank?
  1. Log in to your bank's internet banking (a computer works best; some banking apps can do it too).
  2. Open the account and go to Transactions, Transaction history or Statements.
  3. Choose the dates you want, for example the last 3 to 12 months.
  4. Choose Download or Export and pick CSV. If there is no CSV, choose Excel and save it as CSV.
  5. Add the file here. Several months or accounts at once is fine.

PDF statements cannot be read. Any bank and currency works: the app recognises the columns itself and checks every amount against the bank's balance.

Your files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere. This page even works with your Wi-Fi off.

Getting started

SpendSight reads the statements you download from your bank and shows where your money goes. Everything happens on this device: nothing is uploaded or stored online.

  1. Download a CSV statement from your bank (see Statements for a step-by-step guide).
  2. Add it on the Statements page. Add more months or accounts any time.
  3. Use the menu to explore: start with the Dashboard.

Common questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your statements are read inside this page and never leave your device. Category choices and budgets are saved only in this browser. Closing the page removes the statements.

Why does a payment have the wrong category?

Choose the right one in Transactions (or Top places). The app remembers it for that shop from then on.

What do the small question marks mean?

Hover over one (computer) or tap it (phone) to see what a term means and how it is worked out.

What is "Left over"?

Everything that came in minus everything that went out, including fees, transfers and savings. It equals the change in your bank balance.

Why are transfers and savings shown separately?

Moving money to your own savings or another of your accounts is not spending, so it is not counted as spending. It is still part of "Left over".

How accurate are the numbers?

Every amount is checked against the running balance on your statement. The green box at the top says if everything adds up. Growth projections are estimates based on the return, fee and inflation you choose.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open the SpendSight.html file in your phone's browser and add a statement from your files.

Is this financial advice?

No. SpendSight shows facts from your own statements and simple estimates. For personal advice, speak to a registered financial adviser.
